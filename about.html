About Our Bakery

Welcome to Enike's Bakes nd More, where every cake is baked with love, creativity, and premium ingredients. Our bakery specializes in birthday cakes, wedding cakes, cupcakes, doughnuts, pastries, meat pies, and small chops for every celebration. We are committed to quality, hygiene, affordability, and excellent customer satisfaction. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, wedding, graduation, baby shower, anniversary, or any special event, we are here to make it sweeter.

Our Mission

To provide delicious baked products with exceptional quality, creativity, and excellent customer service while making every celebration memorable.

Our Vision

To become one of Nigeria's most trusted bakery brands, known for quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.
